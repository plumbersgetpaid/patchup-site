Questions

The things people ask first

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use PatchUp — most of it works the way your phone already does, and you can send your first quote within minutes of signing up.

Which trades is it for?

Any of them. Plumbing, electrical, heating, building, roofing, joinery, tiling, landscaping — the admin is the same regardless of what's in the back of the van.

How do customers get quotes and invoices?

By email, as a proper PDF with your business name and logo on it. Replies come straight back to your own email address, so the conversation stays with you.

Can I change the wording of the automatic messages?

Yes. Every automated message — quotes, invoices, reminders, chasers — can be reworded to sound like you rather than like software.

What happens when someone doesn't pay?

PatchUp chases them for you automatically — at 3, 7 and 14 days overdue, in wording you choose. Everything outstanding and everything overdue sits on one screen, so nothing quietly slips.

What about my accountant?

There's a full invoice history you can export whenever you need it.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your settings. No contract, no notice period, no phone call.
